# Mo Research

The research documents for Mo, moved from TheBrain's Mo Research brain into this repo on 23 Sep 2026 (D54, moving research into version control). TheBrain is no longer used. and [design parking lot](https://linear.app/robert-guss/document/design-parking-lot-questions-not-an-execution-queue-86faf283b14a).

research recommendations are not approvals to build.
